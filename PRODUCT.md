# Product

## Register

Product. Contrast Check is a tool; the design serves the task.

## Users

Designers and front-end developers checking a color pair in the middle of other work, often pasting hex values from a design tool, on a laptop or a phone.

## Primary task

Know in seconds whether a text and background pair passes WCAG 2.2, and get an accessible alternative when it does not.

## Principles

- The answer, the contrast ratio, is the single focal point of the page.
- The interface stays readable for any color pair the person chooses.
- No decoration that competes with reading.
- Keyboard and screen reader users get the same result as mouse users.
- One codebase of logic, shared by two framework implementations.

## Tone

Precise, quiet, technical. No marketing copy.

## Anti-references

- The shadcn/ui look and structure. Explicitly banned.
- SaaS hero-metric templates.
- Card grids.
- Glassmorphism.
- Gradient text.
- Dark-mode-by-default "tool aesthetic".
